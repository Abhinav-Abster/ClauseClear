RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (STRICT/ALTERNATIVE)

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES
This Agreement is entered into on January 1, 2025, between Pinnacle Housing Corp ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant"). Landlord leases to Tenant the residential unit at 100 Main Street, Suite 12A, Metropolis ("Premises").

2. LEASE TERM AND AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
The term begins on February 1, 2025, and ends on January 31, 2026. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms at a mandatory 10% rent escalation unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal via certified mail at least ninety (90) days prior to lease end.

3. RENT AND LATE CHARGES
Tenant agrees to pay rent of $2,450.00 per month, strictly due on or before the first (1st) day of each month. No grace period is provided. Payments received after 5:00 PM on the first day incur an immediate late charge of $150.00, plus an additional penalty of $15.00 per day until paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DEDUCTIONS
Tenant shall pay a non-interest-bearing Security Deposit of $4,900.00 (two months' rent). Landlord may retain this deposit for up to sixty (60) days after lease termination. Landlord retains the right to deduct a mandatory non-refundable turnover cleaning fee of $450.00 regardless of premises condition.

5. MAINTENANCE AND TENANT LIABILITY
Tenant shall bear full financial responsibility for all plumbing repairs, drain clogs, appliance repairs, and heating maintenance costing under $500.00 per incident. Landlord disclaims all liability for temporary utility disruptions or AC malfunctions.

6. UNRESTRICTED ACCESS
Landlord and contractor personnel reserve the right to enter the Premises at any time between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM for inspection, maintenance, or marketing, with or without prior notice. Tenant waives statutory 24-hour advance notice requirements.

7. STRICT NO PET POLICY
No animals, birds, or reptiles of any kind are permitted on the Premises under any circumstances. A violation penalty of $500.00 per day shall apply for unauthorized pets.

8. TERMINATION AND ACCELERATION CLAUSE
If Tenant defaults on any covenant or attempts early surrender, Tenant remains liable for the entire remaining rent balance for the full unexpired term, payable immediately. Subletting and assignment are strictly prohibited without Landlord's sole discretionary written consent.

9. BINDING ARBITRATION AND JURY WAIVER
Tenant waives all rights to a trial by jury and agrees that all disputes shall be resolved exclusively through binding private arbitration in New York, NY. Tenant bears all arbitration filing costs.
