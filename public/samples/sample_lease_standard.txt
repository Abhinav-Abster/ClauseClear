RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (STANDARD)

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES
This Agreement is entered into on January 1, 2025, between Oakridge Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant"). Landlord agrees to lease to Tenant the residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apt 4B, Springfield ("Premises").

2. LEASE TERM
The term of this Lease commences on February 1, 2025, and terminates on January 31, 2026 ("Term"). Tenant may renew this Lease for an additional one-year term by providing written notice to Landlord at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration.

3. RENT AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Tenant agrees to pay rent of $2,200.00 per month, due on the first (1st) day of each calendar month. A grace period is provided until the fifth (5th) day of the month. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the fifth (5th) day, a late fee of $50.00 shall be assessed.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,200.00 as a Security Deposit. Landlord shall hold this deposit in an interest-bearing escrow account. Landlord shall return the deposit, less any documented deductions for damage exceeding normal wear and tear, within thirty (30) days following move-out inspection.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and promptly notify Landlord of any defects or needed repairs. Landlord is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing, heating, and major electrical systems. Landlord shall respond to emergency repair requests within 24 hours and non-emergency requests within five (5) business days.

6. ACCESS BY LANDLORD
Landlord or Landlord's designated agents may enter the Premises for inspection, maintenance, or showing to prospective tenants with at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, except in cases of bona fide emergency.

7. PET POLICY
Tenant is permitted to keep one (1) domestic cat or dog weighing under 35 pounds, subject to a one-time refundable pet deposit of $300.00. No additional monthly pet rent shall be charged.

8. TERMINATION AND DEFAULT
If Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice of default, Landlord may initiate eviction proceedings. Tenant may terminate this Lease early due to job relocation outside a 50-mile radius by giving sixty (60) days written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one month's rent.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois.
